## 2 — Design locked by D21; exact candidate review pending

One JournaledHold facade receives the existing real Gate and uses the sole
shipped Journal. The complete baseline carries a hold descriptor with saved
external pins and metadata/path identities; recovery validates them rather
than adopting new pins. Durable close intent precedes publication, a callback
records the full receipt before exclusive drain, and only the original native
watch session can bracket a forward stop/copy. Reattachment always restores
only; it never certifies a lost interval. A private factory may not manufacture
a certifying guard without an actual drain.

Interrupted holds remain failed/unresolved. A matching receipt reattaches only
for restoration. Receipt-gap adoption is restoration-only and requires one unique matching
nonce intent and unchanged saved pins; unmatched marker, metadata or lock refuses.
A known hold found unexpectedly open is explicitly re-closed and drained
before dependency restoration. Its lost interval cannot become accepted copies.
All full-baseline readiness, final physical checks and durable recording must
succeed before a journaled reopen and restored receipt. In particular, reopen
must not precede final physical readiness merely because the service loop passed.
Claude104 accepts this ordering contract. Intent-only/open/ready is ambiguous,
never a proof of publication absence; readiness loss requires protective drain
before any dependency mutation. See D21 for write failures and repeated crashes.

## 3 — Risks

- Publication/receipt crash gaps must not adopt unrelated marker ownership.
- All prior services, including unmutated workers, need readiness before reopen.
- A durable-write failure cannot be converted into a clean restoration receipt.
- A new observer after interruption supplies restoration only, never old continuity.
- Open/broken holds need a fresh drain; a deadline or straggler leaves them closed.
- Gate/source pin substitution refuses; no automatic installation or pin recapture.
- Owned process interruption is not a VM/power-loss durability proof.
- Linux native continuous-watch acceptance remains separately unproved.

## 4 — Verify contract

Use real private copied Gate/Journal consumers and owned foreground/controller
processes. Interrupt before/after publication, before receipt and after drain,
then verify scoped restore-only behavior with immutable pins. Negative controls
cover missing/mismatching/broken markers, stragglers, metadata/pin substitution,
baseline readiness failure and durable-write failures. The same original session
brackets forward operations; a reopened or restoration-only observer cannot
certify them. Reopen follows complete readiness, retains the unresolved fence
on failure and never seals interrupted copies. Root CI plus independent source
challenge are required. The known Mac-specific other-tool harness defaults
remain a parent portability carry-forward, not advertised as CI coverage.
